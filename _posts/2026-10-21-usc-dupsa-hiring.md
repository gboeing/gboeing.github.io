USC's Department of Urban Planning and Spatial Analysis is hiring two
tenure-track assistant professors, one in [transportation planning][1] and one
in [environmental planning][2].

We're looking at these fields broadly. But, for the transportation job, we
particularly encourage applicants whose work uses a modern spatial analytics
toolkit. And for the environmental job, we particularly encourage applicants
whose work intersects with design and the built environment. Overall we seek
creative scholars asking innovative new questions and working/thinking
interdisciplinarily to address problems facing cities on a global stage.

I am the department chair and [Marlon Boarnet][3] is the search chair. We're
available for questions. Come work with us in the living urban laboratory of
sunny, weird, wonderful Los Angeles!
